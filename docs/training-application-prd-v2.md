# Training Application — PRD v2

*Creator Suite · 2026-09-22 · Supersedes v1. Changes: unified Course → Module → Chapter → Item content model with three creation modes (pipeline, manual, AI-generated); quizzes promoted from non-goal to core feature; new Sales & Funnel section (creator-led selling with suite-powered promotion); revised milestones. All courses are sellable regardless of how they were created; generation-specific quality gates are deferred.*

## 1. Summary

The Training Application turns a creator's content into polished, sellable courses, then hosts and sells those courses to students on the platform. Course content arrives three ways — automatically from live sessions (Zoom cloud recordings, uploaded video), manually through a structured outline editor, or generated by AI from a topic prompt — and all three feed one content model, one review step, and one marketplace. It is a managed marketplace: students pay the platform, the platform pays creators and facilitators via percentage revenue shares, and platform-employed facilitators manage the delivery experience.

The app is the third product in the Creator Suite, sharing a media pipeline with the Podcast Clipper and consuming the Social Poster's publishing API for promotion. Its differentiators: "your Zoom recordings become a sellable course automatically," and — unique to the suite — "your course promotes itself": the same pipeline that builds lessons cuts promo clips, and the Poster distributes them with per-clip sales attribution.

## 2. Problem

Subject-matter experts already teach live, but turning recordings into sellable course content requires editing skills, publishing infrastructure, payment handling, and student support most creators don't have. Existing options force a choice: heavyweight LMS platforms (Kajabi, Teachable) that leave all production, quiz-writing, and — critically — all traffic generation to the creator, or agencies that are expensive and slow. And no incumbent closes the loop from content to promotion to sale.

## 3. Goals and Non-Goals

### Goals
- Zero-effort ingestion: a completed Zoom cloud recording becomes a draft course lesson without the creator touching a file.
- Automated production: transcription, dead-air removal, chaptering, titles, descriptions, and thumbnails generated by the media pipeline.
- Structured authoring: creators can build and edit the full course tree by hand — modules, chapters, and typed items (video, text, attachment, quiz) — and mix authored material with pipeline output freely.
- Quizzes as a first-class item type: AI-drafted from each item's content, creator-reviewed, scored on delivery.
- AI-generated trainings: from a topic prompt, generate a course scaffold (Phase A) and, with a research pass, complete text-and-quiz courses (Phase B). Generated courses are sellable like any other.
- Creator-led selling with suite leverage: every published course gets a public sales page with a gated free preview, coupons, and promo clips dispatched through the Social Poster with per-clip sales attribution.
- Simple, reliable delivery: ordered content, secure streaming, progress and quiz-score tracking, magic-link student access.
- Centralized commerce and trustworthy money handling: platform as merchant of record, immutable earnings ledger, versioned splits (including referral parties), automated payouts.
- Facilitator operations: assignment, scoped access, and performance visibility for platform-paid facilitators.

### Non-Goals (v1)
- Creator-branded sites or custom domains (sales pages are platform-hosted).
- Direct student-to-creator payments (Stripe Connect for payouts only).
- Quality gates specific to AI-generated courses — deferred. The interim backstop is the existing machinery: admin-approved creators, the mandatory review-and-approve step before publishing, admin abuse flags, and refunds. Item-level `origin` tracking (FR-21) preserves the option to add gates, disclosure labels, or filtering later without migration.
- Certificates, gamification, or community features (quizzes are in; the rest of the classic LMS layer is not).
- Live streaming or real-time classes.
- Multi-currency pricing.
- Marketplace demand aggregation (browse catalog, recommendations) — an earned feature once creator count justifies it; v1 selling is creator-led.

## 4. Users and Roles

| Role | Who | Primary jobs |
|---|---|---|
| Creator | Subject-matter expert | Connect Zoom, author/review course content, build quizzes, price and publish, promote via the Poster, track earnings |
| Student | End learner | Preview, buy, watch, take quizzes, resume progress, get help from a facilitator |
| Facilitator | Platform-paid delivery manager | Manage assigned courses, support students, monitor completion and quiz outcomes, earn revenue share |
| Admin | Platform operator | Approve creators, set split policies, run payouts, refunds, abuse handling, oversight |

## 5. Product Description

### 5.0 Content Model

A course is a tree: **Course → Module → Chapter → Item**. Items are typed:

- **video** — a rendered lesson (pipeline output or direct upload)
- **text** — rich text/markdown content
- **attachment** — downloadable file
- **quiz** — questions attached to the material around it

Every item records its **origin**: `pipeline` | `manual` | `generated`. The three creation modes below are just three producers writing into this one tree; a single course can mix all three. Course lifecycle stays draft → published → archived; every item passes through the same review-and-approve step before it is publishable.

### 5.1 Creation Modes

**A. Pipeline (Zoom / upload)** — unchanged from v1: Zoom cloud-recording webhook (primary), direct video upload, Google Drive folder watch. Whisper transcription with word-level timestamps; LLM pass produces chapter markers, dead-air/filler cut list, title, description, thumbnail prompts; FFmpeg on GPU workers renders streaming-ready video. Output lands as video items; detected chapter markers map onto the chapter level of the tree.

**B. Manual (outline editor)** — a structured course builder: add, nest, and drag-reorder modules, chapters, and items; write text items in a rich editor; attach video or files per item. This is also how creators interleave written material with recorded lessons.

**C. Generated (AI)** — phased:
- *Phase A — scaffold:* from a topic prompt ("a course on Git for new developers"), generate the module/chapter tree, item stubs with summaries, and draft quizzes. The creator fills stubs with their expertise. Reuses the suite's structured-output LLM pattern.
- *Phase B — full generation:* a research pass (web search + synthesis) fills the scaffold with complete text items and quizzes. The result is an ordinary draft course: the creator reviews, edits, approves, prices, and publishes it like any other. Also the engine for small-business internal training ("generate an onboarding course for my shop").

### 5.2 Quizzes

- An AI pass drafts questions from the surrounding item's transcript or text — multiple-choice and true/false at launch — following the suite's review pattern: generate → creator edits/approves → publish. Regenerate per quiz.
- Creators can also write questions manually (same editor).
- Delivery: students take quizzes in the player; scores recorded per attempt; a course-level setting can require passing a quiz to mark its chapter complete.

### 5.3 Delivery

- **Streaming:** hosted provider (Mux or Cloudflare Stream), signed URLs, no self-hosted video.
- **Student access:** purchase creates an enrollment; magic-link authentication; access enrollment-gated per course.
- **Progress:** per-item completion, resume position, quiz scores; optional pass-to-complete per §5.2.
- **Facilitation:** assigned facilitators with scoped visibility into rosters, completion rates, quiz outcomes, and a student-question inbox.

### 5.4 Sales & Funnel

Creator-led selling, powered by the suite:

- **Public sales page, auto-generated per published course:** hero, curriculum outline (rendered from the course tree), creator bio, price, buy button, and a free preview — a designated item or a pipeline-cut trailer clip.
- **Preview gating & lead capture:** the free preview unlocks with an email address; captured emails feed the creator's launch list (magic-link auth makes email capture native). Waitlist mode for unpublished courses.
- **Offer mechanics:** single price per course plus coupon codes (launch discounts). Cohort windows and bundles are later.
- **The promo flywheel:** publishing a lesson can dispatch promo clips through the Social Poster; every clip carries a tracked link to the sales page, and per-clip attribution ("this TikTok clip sold 4 courses") closes the loop.
- **Referrals via the ledger:** an affiliate is one more party type in a split policy at N basis points, attributed by referral link — no new financial machinery.

### 5.5 Commerce and Payouts

- Platform is merchant of record. Checkout via Stripe (with Stripe Tax) or a merchant-of-record provider; decision tracked as an open question.
- Every sale records gross, tax, and processor fees; splits computed on **net** in basis points.
- Split policies versioned as complete sets per course (creator + facilitator(s) + optional affiliate + platform = 100%); changes apply forward only.
- Immutable append-only earnings ledger; refunds are negative mirror entries; rounding remainders absorbed by the platform share.
- Payout runs sweep unpaid balances per party and transfer via Stripe Connect Express.

## 6. Key User Flows

- **Creator onboarding:** apply → admin approves → connect Zoom + Stripe Connect → first recording ingested automatically.
- **Recording to lesson:** Zoom webhook → pipeline job → draft video items → creator review/edit → approve → placed in the course tree.
- **Manual authoring:** creator builds the outline → writes text items, attaches video → generates quizzes per item → reviews → publishes.
- **Generated course:** creator prompts a topic → scaffold (or full generation with research) → creator reviews/edits/approves → prices → publishes; sellable like any course.
- **Sell:** publish → sales page live → creator dispatches promo clips via the Poster → visitor unlocks preview with email → buys → enrollment + ledger splits + magic-link welcome.
- **Learn:** student watches, takes quizzes, resumes; completion tracked; facilitator handles questions.
- **Payout / Refund:** unchanged from v1 (scheduled runs / negative mirror entries, enrollment revocation configurable).

## 7. Functional Requirements

### Ingestion & Production (pipeline)
- **FR-01** — Zoom OAuth connection per creator; cloud-recording-completed webhooks trigger ingestion with no user action.
- **FR-02** — Direct upload of video (mp4/mov) and audio (mp3/m4a) up to 4 hours.
- **FR-03** — Whisper transcription with word-level timestamps; transcript editable and stored per item.
- **FR-04** — LLM production pass returns validated structured JSON: chapters, cut list, title, description, thumbnail prompt; invalid output retried with error feedback.
- **FR-05** — GPU render workers apply cuts, captions, intro/outro; job status visible; failed jobs retryable.
- **FR-06** — Creator review screen to edit chapters/title/description and approve or re-render before an item becomes publishable.

### Authoring & Content Model
- **FR-20** — Outline editor: create, nest, and drag-reorder modules, chapters, and items across the course tree.
- **FR-21** — Typed items (video, text, attachment, quiz); rich-text editing for text items; per-item video/file attach; every item records origin (`pipeline` | `manual` | `generated`).
- **FR-22** — Quiz generation from an item's transcript or text (multiple-choice, true/false); creator edits, regenerates, approves; manual question authoring in the same editor.
- **FR-23** — Course scaffold generation from a topic prompt: module/chapter tree, item stubs with summaries, draft quizzes.
- **FR-24** — Full course generation: research pass fills the scaffold with complete text items and quizzes; result is a draft course subject to the standard review-and-approve flow.

### Courses & Delivery
- **FR-07** — Course CRUD over the tree model: pricing, draft/published/archived status; mixed-origin content in one course.
- **FR-08** — Streaming via signed URLs; playback gated by enrollment.
- **FR-09** — Magic-link student authentication; students see only enrolled, published courses.
- **FR-10** — Per-item completion, resume position, and quiz attempt/score tracking; optional pass-to-complete per course.
- **FR-11** — Facilitator assignment per course; facilitators see only assigned courses, rosters, progress, and quiz outcomes.
- **FR-12** — Student question inbox routed to the course's facilitators.

### Sales & Funnel
- **FR-25** — Auto-generated public sales page per published course: hero, curriculum from the tree, creator bio, price, buy button, free preview.
- **FR-26** — Preview gating: free preview unlocks with email capture; emails feed the creator's list; waitlist mode pre-publish.
- **FR-27** — Coupon codes: percentage or fixed discount, expiry, usage caps; applied at checkout and recorded on the sale.
- **FR-28** — Tracked promo links: clips dispatched via the Social Poster carry attribution parameters; per-clip and per-platform sales attribution reported to the creator.
- **FR-29** — Referral/affiliate party type in split policies (basis points), attributed via referral links, settled through the standard ledger.

### Commerce, Ledger & Payouts
- **FR-13** — Checkout collects payment to the platform account; sale records gross, tax, processor fee, net, currency, processor reference, and any coupon/referral attribution.
- **FR-14** — Successful payment atomically creates enrollment and writes split ledger entries from the policy set effective at sale time.
- **FR-15** — Split policies stored in basis points, versioned as complete sets summing to exactly 100%; admin-managed.
- **FR-16** — Ledger entries append-only; the only permitted mutation is one-time payout assignment; refunds create negative mirror entries.
- **FR-17** — Payout runs sweep positive unpaid balances per party, create payout records, execute Stripe Connect transfers; concurrent runs cannot double-pay.
- **FR-18** — Creators, facilitators, and affiliates see earnings statements: per-sale entries, balances, payout history.
- **FR-19** — Admin console: creator approval, split management, refunds, payout oversight, facilitator performance, abuse flags and takedowns.

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-01** — Row-level security enforces the role model on every table; money tables accept writes only via service role or security-definer functions.
- **NFR-02** — Financial invariants enforced in the database (split sums, ledger immutability, payout locking), not application code.
- **NFR-03** — Render workers scale independently of web infrastructure; a 60-minute recording renders in under 30 minutes.
- **NFR-04** — Pipeline and generation jobs are idempotent and resumable; a failed step never duplicates ledger entries, enrollments, or renders.
- **NFR-05** — All video delivery via signed, expiring URLs; sales-page preview items are the only publicly reachable content, and only post-email-gate.
- **NFR-06** — Webhook handlers (Zoom, payments) verified, idempotent, safe under retries.
- **NFR-07** — Generation COGS (LLM + research calls) tracked per course and surfaced in admin views.

## 9. System Architecture

Monorepo (pnpm) shared across the Creator Suite:

- **packages/core** — tenancy, auth, billing primitives, shared UI.
- **packages/media-pipeline** — job queue (pg-boss/BullMQ), ingest adapters (Zoom, upload), Whisper, LLM steps, FFmpeg render dispatch to GPU worker pool.
- **apps/trainer** — this product: content tree + three creation heads (pipeline, outline editor, generation), quiz engine, delivery, sales pages & funnel, commerce, facilitator tooling.
- **apps/poster** — consumed via internal API for promo dispatch; tracked links feed attribution (FR-28).

Platform services: Supabase (Postgres, auth, storage, RLS), Stripe (checkout, Tax, Connect payouts), Mux or Cloudflare Stream, GPU render workers (Modal / dedicated boxes), LLM + web-research provider for generation. v1 database migrations (roles, courses, sales, splits, ledger, payouts) need a revision for the tree content model, item origin, quizzes, coupons, and the affiliate party type.

## 10. Milestones

| Milestone | Scope | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| M1 — Creation | Zoom ingest, pipeline, render, review, outline editor, quiz generation | A real Zoom recording becomes an approved lesson; a creator hand-builds a mixed course (video + text + quiz) with no operator help |
| M2 — Delivery | Courses, enrollment, streaming, progress, quiz taking, magic-link auth | A test student purchases (test mode), watches, passes a quiz, and resumes end-to-end |
| M3 — Marketplace & Funnel | Live payments, ledger, splits, facilitator tooling, payouts, sales pages, preview gating, coupons | First live sale lands through a public sales page; ledger entries correct; first payout reconciles to the cent |
| M4 — Promotion | Social Poster integration, promo clip generation, tracked-link attribution | A published lesson dispatches clips and the creator sees clip-attributed sales |
| M5 — Generation | Scaffold generation, then research-backed full generation; referral splits | A prompted topic becomes a published, purchasable course after one review pass |

## 11. Success Metrics

- Time from recording end to approved lesson (target: one creator touch, under 10 minutes of review).
- Creator activation: % of approved creators with a published course within 30 days.
- Sales-page conversion: visitor → preview email → purchase.
- Promo attribution: % of sales attributed to Poster-dispatched clips.
- Quiz adoption: % of published courses containing approved quizzes.
- Generation usage: scaffolds started → published generated courses.
- GMV, take rate, net revenue per course.

## 12. Open Questions

- **OQ-1** — Checkout rails: Stripe + Stripe Tax vs. a merchant-of-record provider (carried from v1).
- **OQ-2** — Generated-course economics: does Phase B generation meter against the creator's plan (research + LLM cost), and is there a per-course generation fee?
- **OQ-3** — Disclosure: label generated courses on the sales page now, or defer with the quality gates? (Origin tracking makes either possible later.)
- **OQ-4** — Default affiliate share (basis points) and whether creators can set it per course.
- **OQ-5** — Email list ownership and export: captured preview emails belong to the creator — export rights, unsubscribe handling, and whether the platform can also mail them.
- **OQ-6** — Marketplace catalog trigger: what creator/course count justifies building browse + recommendations?
